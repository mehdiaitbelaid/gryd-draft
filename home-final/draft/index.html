Build homes that power themselves.

Solar may be the new standard, but not all solar is smart. With a fully managed, software-powered model, Gryd delivers high-performance solar systems that work better for homeowners, meet the next wave of regulations, and support the energy system of tomorrow.

For developers

  • £0 hardware cost. Gryd funds all solar panel, battery, inverter and monitoring hardware.
  • Future Homes Standard compliance. Gryd systems meet and typically exceed the FHS on-site renewable generation requirement.
  • SAP uplift of 10 to 20 points, typically an EPC improvement of at least one level.
  • No increase in build cost, so homes stay affordable and your margins don't get squeezed.
  • Deployable to all unit types. Gryd won't cherry-pick only the best plots like some others, we can deploy smart solar to every unit.

For homeowners

  • Lower energy bills from day one, typically 20 to 30%.
  • Significant lifetime savings, up to £50,000 over the 25-year life of the system.
  • Fixed, inflation-proof subscription, locked in for 25 years.
  • 25-year Gryd Guarantee with zero maintenance burden, covering insurance, 24/7 monitoring, servicing and maintenance.
  • Flexibility and choice. Homeowners can subscribe to the system or buy it outright at any time for a transparent price that reduces over time.
