Step 2 of 4 · Electricity use

Electricity use

One figure for the year. All four columns are priced on it, so the way it was arrived at is printed in full rather than summarised.

How the year’s use is arrived at

Every plot here is assumed to have a heat pump, so the heat pump ladder is the only one used. The ladder sets the year’s total; the half hourly shape underneath is Elexon Profile Class 1, which carries no heating season.

DPER is the delivered energy rate for regulated energy only: space heating, hot water, ventilation, pumps, fans and fixed lighting. Appliances, cooking and everything else a household plugs in are outside it, which is what the unregulated figure below is for.

kWh/m2

From the plot’s SAP assessment, in kWh per square metre per year.

m2

The floor area the assessment used, not the ground floor area recorded on screen one.

kWh

The year’s allowance for everything the SAP assessment does not cover.
